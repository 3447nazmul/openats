Senior Full Stack Engineer (Remote)

Required Qualifications & Skills:
• 4+ years of professional full-stack software development experience.
• Strong proficiency in Python, TypeScript, and JavaScript.
• Hands-on experience with React, Next.js, FastAPI, and PostgreSQL.
• Solid understanding of cloud infrastructure on AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD pipelines.
• Experience with Kafka, distributed systems, and GraphQL is a strong plus.
• Excellent communication, leadership, and problem-solving skills.
